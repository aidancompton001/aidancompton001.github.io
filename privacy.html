Privacy policy

CrossPostBot is used only by its owner. It requests the Google scope drive.file, which gives access only to files the app itself creates: one spreadsheet with the owner's listings and a folder with item photos and database backups.

The app does not read, share or sell any other Google data. No data is transferred to third parties. Data stays in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's server.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: via the Google account that owns this app.
